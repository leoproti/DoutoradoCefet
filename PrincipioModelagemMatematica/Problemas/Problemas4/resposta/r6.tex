O peso em altitude $h$ é
\[
W(h)=10\ \text{N}\left(\frac{R}{R+h}\right)^{2},
\]
com $R\approx6{,}371\times10^{6}\ \mathrm{m}$. Assim,
\[
W(10)\approx10\left(\frac{6{,}371\times10^{6}}{6{,}371\times10^{6}+10}\right)^{2}\approx9{,}99997\ \mathrm{N},
\]
\[
W(8848)\approx10\left(\frac{6{,}371\times10^{6}}{6{,}371\times10^{6}+8848}\right)^{2}\approx9{,}9723\ \mathrm{N}.
\]
