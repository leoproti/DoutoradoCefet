Para um pêndulo, $T\propto\sqrt{L}$. Assim:
\begin{align*}
L\to L/2&:\quad T\to T/\sqrt{2}\approx0{,}707T \Rightarrow \text{queda de }29{,}3\%,\\
L\to \frac{2}{3}L&:\quad T\to \sqrt{2/3}T\approx0{,}816T \Rightarrow \text{queda de }18{,}4\%,\\
L\to L/3&:\quad T\to T/\sqrt{3}\approx0{,}577T \Rightarrow \text{queda de }42{,}3\%.
\end{align*}
