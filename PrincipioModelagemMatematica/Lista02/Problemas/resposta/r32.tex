Escreva
\[
f(r)=\sqrt{a^2+r^2}=r\sqrt{1+\frac{a^2}{r^2}}.
\]
Para $r\gg a$, o termo $a^2/r^2$ e pequeno, e podemos usar a expansao binomial
\[
(1+u)^{1/2} \approx 1 + \frac{u}{2} - \frac{u^2}{8} + \cdots.
\]
Com $u=a^2/r^2$,
\[
f(r) \approx r\left(1+\frac{a^2}{2r^2}-\frac{a^4}{8r^4}+\cdots\right)
= r + \frac{a^2}{2r} - \frac{a^4}{8r^3} + \cdots.
\]
Assim, a aproximacao de campo distante e
\[
\sqrt{a^2+r^2} \approx r + \frac{a^2}{2r}.
\]
