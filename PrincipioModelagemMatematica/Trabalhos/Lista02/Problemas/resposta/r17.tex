Para ordem de dois termos, as expansoes pedidas sao:
\[
\sin x \approx x - \frac{x^3}{6},
\]
\[
\cos x \approx 1 - \frac{x^2}{2},
\]
\[
1-\sin x \approx 1 - x + \frac{x^3}{6},
\]
\[
1-\cos x \approx \frac{x^2}{2} - \frac{x^4}{24}
\approx \frac{x^2}{2}.
\]

Logo, os elementos da tabela ficam:
\[
\sin x:~x-\frac{x^3}{6},\quad
\cos x:~1-\frac{x^2}{2},\quad
1-\sin x:~1-x+\frac{x^3}{6},\quad
1-\cos x:~\frac{x^2}{2}-\frac{x^4}{24}.
\]
